# Event Trigger Rules

## Fire
- Trigger after 3 consecutive frames
- Priority: HIGH

## Smoke
- Trigger after 5 consecutive frames
- Priority: MEDIUM

## Person
- Trigger immediately
- Priority: CRITICAL

## Landslide
- Trigger after 2 consecutive frames
- Priority: HIGH

## Flood
- Trigger after 2 consecutive frames
- Priority: HIGH

## Debris
- Trigger after 3 consecutive frames
- Priority: LOW
